## Daily workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check service health">
    Call `GET /health` and verify process uptime.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review state">
    Call `GET /state` and inspect USD balance, USDT balance, realized profit, and open tranches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm automatic monitoring">
    The service starts the quote stream and trading loop on deployment. Check `GET /auto/status` and `GET /broker/quote?source=websocket`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review logs">
    Call `GET /logs?limit=100` and check for repeated errors or unexpected decisions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust config only when needed">
    Update thresholds deliberately and record the reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to watch

- Too many open tranches means price kept falling and inventory is accumulating.
- No sells for a long time means the exit threshold may be too high for current volatility.
- Frequent buys and sells with tiny profit can still lose money if real fees are higher than assumed.
- A `warming_up` WebSocket quote means the stream has started but no fresh book ticker has arrived yet.
- Gaps in logs may indicate process restarts or storage issues.

## Pause procedure

For the current paper prototype:

```bash
sudo systemctl stop open-arbitrage-tool
```

For a future live broker, pause should also cancel open exchange orders and write a final state snapshot.
